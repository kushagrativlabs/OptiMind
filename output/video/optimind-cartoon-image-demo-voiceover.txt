Welcome to OptiMind’s local image generation experience.

This demo starts a fresh conversation and asks for a photorealistic real-world scene: a fluffy orange cat sitting by a sunlit window in a warm home interior.

The prompt is entered naturally in the same chat. OptiMind recognizes the image request, sends it to the local image generator, and shows the result when it is ready.

The finished image appears directly in the conversation, with a download option so it can be saved and shared.

This keeps image creation simple: describe the idea, wait for the local render, and continue the conversation when you are ready.
