Welcome to OptiMind, a private AI workspace for turning website data into clear growth actions.

Everything starts in one focused workspace. OptiMind runs locally with Ollama, so your conversations and website information stay under your control.

In the Websites area, each client site gets its own workspace. You can connect a public website, configure the WordPress REST API when available, choose the right local model, and keep the work organized from the beginning.

Now we move to the conversation view. Instead of opening several tools, your team can ask one simple question and receive a useful answer in plain language. OptiMind can use the local model for everyday reasoning and automatically bring in web search when current information is required.

This is a live Tivlabs homepage snapshot with a clear auditor overview: the target, scope, completion status, and exact evidence collected. The assistant explains what was verified, what still needs a full crawl, and how to improve SEO, answer-engine visibility, and generative-search visibility with practical steps a client can understand.

The website workspace is where the research becomes measurable. The live snapshot checks titles, descriptions, headings, canonical URLs, robots directives, structured data, images, and indexability signals, while clearly marking page-speed and full-site findings as not measured.

The report is designed to answer three questions: what is wrong, why it matters, and what should be fixed first. Page-speed measurements and other limitations are clearly labeled instead of being guessed.

The demo then moves into a live chat turn. A user types a question, OptiMind streams its response in the conversation, and the input remains available for the next question just like a modern chat assistant.

From there, the team can move from findings to action. Every website, model, conversation, and recommendation stays in one place, making follow-up work easier and more consistent.

When the review is complete, OptiMind produces a professional report that can be shared with the client. The result is a repeatable process: discover, understand, prioritize, improve, and report.

OptiMind gives teams a practical way to combine private local AI with website intelligence, without losing control of their data or their workflow.
